# Kupro — Vision

## TL;DR

> The whole team (developers, sales and their agents) keeps every task, bug and plan in one place that's quick to use, and it gets
> tracked as you talk, not as extra work. Hosted in the EU or on your own servers.

Short form: *Talk it through. Kupro keeps the tracker up to date.*

## Commitments

1. **Faster, easier issue tracking.** Epics, features, bugs and tasks take fewer clicks and less waiting than in the
   tools teams use today.
2. **One tool for the whole team.** Developers and sales work in the same place instead of splitting between, say,
   Azure DevOps and Trello. Sales needs no separate concepts: labels and views cover it.
3. **Connect your agent.** Anything a person can do, an agent can do too ([ADR-001](adr/ADR-001-agent-parity.md)).
4. **Less manual work.** Every feature should remove a step, not add one.
5. **EU-hosted, self-hostable.** Your data stays in Europe or on your own servers
   ([ADR-006](adr/ADR-006-eu-vendor-policy.md)).
6. **Talk to it.** Speaking is the lowest-friction input: say it in a meeting and the tracker follows.

## One language, kept small

Kupro must stay lean and easy to learn. Everyone, in every role, uses the same few words for the same things: in the
UI, API, CLI, MCP, docs and marketing. A new need is met with what exists (labels, views, fields) before a new concept
is added. The words are listed in the glossary in [branding.md](branding.md).
