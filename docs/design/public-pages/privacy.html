Privacy policy

Last updated [1 December 2026] · Dansk version

Draft for legal review. Placeholders are highlighted. Describes Flowstate as built in October 2026, with transcripts deleted when a meeting ends.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to run your account and your workspace. Nothing for advertising.
  • Everything is stored and processed in the EU, by EU companies.
  • Meeting audio is never stored. Transcripts are deleted when the meeting ends, unless your workspace chooses to keep them.
  • We don't use your data to train AI models, and neither do our vendors.
  • We use no tracking cookies. You can see, export and delete your data.

Your data or your company's

Flowstate holds two kinds of personal data, and the rules differ.

  • Data about you as a user: your account, how you sign in, invoices, support emails and security logs. We decide how this is used, so we are the data controller. This policy covers it.
  • Data inside a workspace: issues, comments, attachments, meeting transcripts and everything else your team puts in. The company that owns the workspace decides what goes in and who sees it. They are the controller and we are their data processor, bound by our data processing agreement. Questions about that data go to your workspace owner first; we help them answer.

Meeting agent

The meeting agent is off until someone in your workspace starts a meeting session. While it runs:

  • Your browser records short pieces of speech, a few seconds each, and sends them to our servers.
  • We send each piece to Scaleway in Paris for transcription, then drop the audio. Audio is held in memory only and never written to disk.
  • We send the text, with the titles of issues that might be relevant, to a language model at Scaleway to suggest issue changes.
  • The transcript is kept while the meeting runs, so the agent can follow the conversation. Each suggestion shows the few words that triggered it, so you can see why it was proposed. When the meeting ends, the transcript and those words are deleted. If nobody ends the meeting, they are deleted after a day.
  • A workspace owner can choose to keep transcripts for up to 365 days instead, for example to look back at a planning meeting. Everyone in a session then sees how long the transcript is kept.

Everyone in a session sees that it is listening. If people who are not Flowstate users are in the meeting, the workspace owner is responsible for telling them and, where needed, asking for their consent. The person who starts the session can pause or stop it at any time.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of EU companies to run Flowstate. They process data only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement. The full list, with what each one does, is on the sub-processors page.

  • OVHcloud (France): servers, database, file storage and backups, in EU data centres.
  • Scaleway (France): sending email, and transcription and suggestions for the meeting agent.
  • Alunta (Denmark) and our card acquirer [OnPay / QuickPay] (Denmark): subscriptions and payments.

We don't transfer personal data outside the EU/EEA. Our source code is hosted on GitHub; no customer data is stored there. We share data with public authorities only when Danish or EU law requires it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no selling or renting data, no data brokers.
  • No tracking cookies or third-party analytics. See the cookie policy.
  • No training of AI models on your data. Scaleway processes meeting audio and text to return a result. It doesn't keep what we send or use it to train or improve models. The one exception: if traffic harms their service, Scaleway may keep the affected requests for up to two weeks to investigate. It keeps anonymous usage statistics (such as request counts) for up to 6 months.
  • No automated decisions with legal or similar effect on you. Meeting suggestions are applied only when your workspace allows it, and can always be undone.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS). Passwords are hashed by Keycloak, the open-source sign-in server we host ourselves. Access to production is limited to [named people] and protected with two-factor authentication. Each workspace's data is separated in the database, and every query is checked against the workspace you are signed in to. If a breach affects your data, we tell the workspace owner without undue delay, and Datatilsynet within 72 hours where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or give it to you in a machine-readable format. You can object to processing based on legitimate interest. Write to [privacy@domain]. For data inside a workspace, we pass your request to the workspace owner and help them handle it.

Much of this you can do yourself: edit your profile in the app, export a workspace (owners) [export not built yet], or delete your account.

If you think we handle your data wrongly, you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency: Datatilsynet, Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, dt@datatilsynet.dk, datatilsynet.dk. We would like the chance to fix it first.

Changes

When we change this policy in a way that matters, we email workspace owners at least 30 days before it takes effect and show a note in the app. Earlier versions are kept at [domain]/privacy/history.
