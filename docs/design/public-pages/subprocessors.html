What we don't use

No US cloud providers, no third-party analytics, no advertising networks, no error-tracking SaaS. Logs, traces and metrics run on our own servers. Our source code is on GitHub, which stores no customer data.

Changes

Before we add or replace a sub-processor, we email workspace owners at least 30 days ahead and update this page. If you object for a reasonable data protection reason, tell us at [privacy@domain] within those 30 days. We will try to find a solution; if we can't, you can end your subscription and we refund prepaid fees for the remaining period.

Want the email? Workspace owners get it automatically. Others can subscribe at [privacy@domain].
