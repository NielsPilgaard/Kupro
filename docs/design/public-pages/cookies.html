Cookies

Last updated [1 December 2026] · Dansk version

The short version

  • We only store what you need to sign in and stay signed in.
  • No tracking, no analytics, no advertising cookies, and nothing from other companies.
  • That's why you don't see a cookie banner.

Why there's no banner

Danish rules on cookies (cookiebekendtgørelsen) require consent for storing information in your browser, except when it is strictly necessary for a service you asked for. Everything Kupro stores is needed to sign you in and keep your session secure, so no consent is needed. If we ever add something that does need consent, we will ask first, with "Reject" as easy as "Accept".
