Pricing ideas

All four fit ADR-013 (workspace is the billing unit, a seat is an active human Owner or Member) except D's bands, which still count seats but bill a band. Prices are excluding Danish VAT (moms). EUR shown, with DKK at roughly 7.46.

  • Market: Linear Basic $10, Business $16 per user a month (annual). Jira Standard $8.15, Premium $17.
  • Agents are free in every model (ADR-013 OQ-2 leaning). Self-hosted stays free under AGPL.

Chosen

One paid plan after a 30-day trial. Everything is in it, including the meeting agent. Easiest to explain, easiest to build in Alunta.

Cookie banner

Danish rules (cookiebekendtgørelsen, under ePrivacy) require consent only for storage that isn't strictly necessary. Flowstate today sets only Keycloak's sign-in cookies, and the web app keeps the sign-in token in session storage. Both are strictly necessary, so no banner is required.

  • Recommended: option 1. No banner, a short line in the footer and a cookie page. It is also a selling point.
  • If you ever add analytics, use a cookieless EU tool and stay on option 1, or switch to option 3. Datatilsynet expects "Reject" to be as easy as "Accept".

Legal pages

English drafts in plain language, one version each. They describe how Flowstate works today, with one change you asked for: no transcript retention. Danish versions follow once the English text is settled.

  • Have a Danish lawyer or your accountant's legal partner review before the first external user (manual step E2/E3).
  • The DPA uses Datatilsynet's standard clauses with Flowstate's appendices. Lawyers in Denmark know them, which keeps review short.
