AI connections: three directions

Workspace settings → AI, where an Owner or a member with billing access connects the workspace's own Scaleway key or EU endpoint (ADR-022). The directions differ in how much of the routing they show and what leads the page. Nothing leaves the page: checking a key is simulated.

01

One card

The fast path first: a short guide, paste the key, Connect. Routes, models and custom endpoints sit under "Advanced". Least to read; the routing is hidden until someone wants it.

 02

Connections and routes

The model of ADR-022, shown as it is: connection cards (Kupro's included one, your Scaleway key, your own endpoint) and a table of what runs where, per language too. Most flexible, and most to take in.

 03

Usage first

Leads with this month's hours and who paid for them. "Go unlimited" opens a three-step wizard: make a key, paste and check, choose what runs on it. Sells the why; routing reduced to three checkboxes.

Try in each

  • Connect a key: any text works; a key containing "bad" is rejected with Scaleway's 401.
  • Set State to "Own key rejected": what the Owner is told, and that Kupro does not fall back to its own key.
  • Add your own EU endpoint (a self-hosted Whisper). Try http://… or https://10.0.0.5/v1: both are refused. The EU confirmation is required.
  • Change a model away from Kupro's default: it's marked "Not tested by Kupro".
  • Switch Viewing as to Member: read-only, with who to ask.
  • Disconnect: back to the included hours.
