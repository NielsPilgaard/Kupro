Time registration: three directions

Every entry is on an issue (ADR-021). The directions differ in how a member logs and reviews their week. The issue panel, the Log time dialog, Team time with the export (switch "Viewing as" to Owner) and Settings are the same in all three.

01

Week grid

A timesheet: a row per issue, a column per day. Type durations straight into cells, Tab and arrows to move. Day totals at the bottom. Best for people who fill in the week in one go.

02

Day journal

A strip of the week's day totals, and one day's entries below. One line logs: "1h30 KUP-142 note". Best for logging as you go, a few times a day.

03

Log where you work

Logging lives on the issue (T) and in the command palette ("log 1h30 KUP-142"). My time is a list of days to check and fix, with days that have nothing logged marked.

Try in each

  • Log time on KUP-142, then see it on My time.
  • Open KUP-138 (Done): its time is locked.
  • On KUP-142, set the state to Done: log the last hours and mark it Done in one save.
  • On the Board, drag KUP-120 (no time from you) to Done: it asks for time. Drag KUP-147 (has time): it moves straight away.
  • Viewing as Owner: the issue shows the total and each person's share; Team time and Export appear.
  • Durations: 1h30, 90m, 1,5, 2.
