Time registration

Members log the time they spend on issues. Danish employers must record working time; this is where your team does it. Off by default.

  • Each person sees only their own time. Owners see everyone's and export it.
  • Time on a Done or Canceled issue is locked until the issue is reopened.
  • Records are kept for 5 years, also after someone leaves, and also if you turn this off.
