Voice control: three directions

Picked 2026-11-27: 01 and 03, as each person's choice. 01 is the default; onboarding asks whether you want to use voice, and yes switches to 03. Push to talk anywhere in Kupro. One utterance changes issues, goes somewhere, or dictates into the field you're in (task 036). The directions differ in where the button lives and where results appear. Nothing is recorded here: holding the button plays back the phrase picked in "Say" in the prototype bar.

01

Top bar mic (picked, the default)

A Talk button next to search on every screen, plus hold Ctrl+Space. Results drop down as a card under the top bar; Enter applies. Compact and familiar. On a phone the button is small and at the top of the screen.

 02

Voice in the command palette

The palette is the one place for commands, typed or spoken. Hold Ctrl+Space or the mic in the palette; the result shows as palette rows you run with Enter. Typing the same sentence works too. Dictation has its own mic in each text field.

 03

Thumb button (picked, by choice)

Built for the field and for people who'd rather not type: a large round button within thumb reach on every screen. Results open in a bottom sheet with big buttons and large text. On desktop the sheet docks bottom right.

Try in each

  • Say "Move KUP-12 to next sprint" (the default): a card with the diff, then Apply and Undo.
  • Say "Give the dark mode one to Sofie…": two changes on KUP-38 in one go. Untick one before applying.
  • Say the Danish phrase: the same, in Danish.
  • Say "Mark the login bug as done": two issues match, so it asks which.
  • Say "Open KUP-41" or "Show my issues in the next sprint": it goes there at once, with nothing to confirm.
  • Open KUP-12, click into the comment box, then talk: the words go in at the cursor, and "Tidy up?" offers spelling and punctuation fixes.
  • Turn on "Apply without asking when sure", then say the sprint phrase again: it applies at once, with Undo. Creating an issue still asks.
  • Set AI to "Kupro hours spent" or "Own key rejected", and switch Viewing as to Member: what each person is told.
  • Device: Phone 360.

Hotkey: hold Ctrl+Space; the final key is still to be decided. A tap starts and a second tap stops, for anyone who can't hold a button.
