Three steps

  1. 1
    Make a tokenCreate an API token on your Atlassian account's security page. You paste it with your email.Create a personal access token in your Jira profile. Behind a VPN? Run kupro import jira --url https://jira.example.com --local instead; the token stays on your machine.
  2. 2
    RehearseIn Kupro, Import → Jira. Paste your site address and token, check the mapping, and see a dry run before anything is written.
  3. 3
    Import, then switch when readyKupro re-syncs every hour, so the team can keep working in Jira. Press Finish on the day you move.

What changes

One workflow

Backlog, Todo, In Progress, In Review, Done, Canceled. Your custom statuses map into these.

No custom fields

Their values stay readable in a details block on each issue, and their history as source changes.

No time tracking

Logged work is summed into the details block.

Service Management

Request types, queues and SLAs stay in Jira.

Four levels (Initiatives)

Kupro has three levels. Keep Epics as Epics and relate each Initiative to them, or make Initiatives the Epics. The dry run shows both.
