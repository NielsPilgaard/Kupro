Switch from Jira

Bring your Jira projects. Keep the history.

Kupro imports from Jira Cloud and Data Center: issues, sprints, comments, files and who changed what. Rehearse it on real data, keep working in Jira, and switch on the day that suits you.

28 March 2029
Jira Data Center support ends on 28 March 2029.Server ended in 2024. Kupro reads both, so you can move before the date instead of on it.

What comes across

  • ✓
    Projects and keysPROJ-123 stays PROJ-123. A key longer than ten letters gets a shorter one, and the old key keeps working as an alias.
  • ✓
    Epics, stories and sub-tasksEpic > Story > Sub-task lands as Epic > Feature > Task. Initiatives: you choose how the four levels fold.
  • ✓
    Statuses and prioritiesEach status maps by its category into Kupro's workflow. You see the mapping and can change it.
  • ✓
    Sprints and versionsSprints keep their dates. Versions with a release date become milestones on the delivery plan.
  • ✓
    Comments, attachments, linksMentions, links to other issues and inline images are rewritten to point inside Kupro.
  • ✓
    HistoryStatus, assignee, sprint and priority changes show with the original person and time.
  • ✓
    Labels and componentsLabels stay labels. Components become labels too, unless you untick them.

What changes

  • →
    One workflowBacklog, Todo, In Progress, In Review, Done, Canceled. Your custom statuses map into these.
  • →
    No custom fieldsTheir values stay readable in a details block on each issue, and their history as source changes.
  • →
    No time trackingLogged work is summed into the details block.
  • →
    Service ManagementRequest types, queues and SLAs stay in Jira.

Three steps

  1. Make a tokenJira Cloud: an API token from your Atlassian account. Data Center: a personal access token.
  2. RehearseIn Kupro, Import → Jira. Paste your site address and token, check the mapping, and see a dry run before anything is written.
  3. Import, then switch when readyKupro re-syncs every hour, so the team can keep working in Jira. Press Finish on the day you move.
Jira behind a VPN? Run the import on your own machine; the token never leaves it:
kupro import jira --url https://jira.example.com --local
