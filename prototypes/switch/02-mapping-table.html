Three steps

STEP 1Make a tokenJira Cloud: an API token from your Atlassian account. Data Center: a personal access token.
STEP 2RehearseIn Kupro, Import → Jira. Paste your site address and token, check the mapping, and see a dry run before anything is written.
STEP 3Import, then switch when readyKupro re-syncs every hour, so the team can keep working in Jira. Press Finish on the day you move.

Behind a VPN? kupro import jira --url https://jira.example.com --local runs it on your machine; the token stays there.
